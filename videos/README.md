# Snoring videos

Two narrated, animated videos about what you can do at home about snoring, based on published research.

| Video | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [`out/snoring-explained.mp4`](out/snoring-explained.mp4) | 6:15 | **Why we snore, and what actually helps at home.** How the airway narrows and vibrates in sleep, what makes it worse, and the evidence for each home fix: exercises, sleeping on your side, alcohol, weight and exercise, nose and smoking, didgeridoo and singing, why to skip mouth tape, and when to see a doctor. Ends with the list of sources. |
| [`out/snoring-exercises.mp4`](out/snoring-exercises.mp4) | 10:16 | **Follow-along exercise routine.** The five mouth and throat exercises from the 2015 snoring trial, plus two optional extras, in real time. Every repetition is counted aloud and timed on screen, the 30-second hold counts down, and rests show what's next. Do it three times a day. |

Both have English subtitles (soft subtitles in the MP4, and `.srt` files next to them).

[RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) lists every study used, the exact numbers shown, and caveats.

> General information, not medical advice. Loud snoring with breathing pauses, gasping or daytime sleepiness can be sleep apnea. See a doctor.

## What's in each video

**Explainer.** An animated side cutaway of the head shows air flowing through the nose and throat. As the person falls asleep, the tongue and soft palate sag back, the airway narrows and the soft palate flutters, which is the snore. Then:

- the head rolls onto its back to show gravity
- alcohol relaxes the throat further
- extra tissue builds up around the airway
- the nose lining swells

Each fix gets its study, sample size and result on screen.

**Exercise routine.** Each exercise shows:

- an **inside view**: a cutaway of the tongue, soft palate or cheek muscle moving, with the working muscle highlighted
- a **mirror view** of what it looks like from the outside
- a ring timer with the repetition count, a phase label ("Slide back", "Press", "AH!", "Squeeze in") and a bar that times each movement

A 3-2-1 countdown starts each exercise, and a rest screen previews the next one.

## How it's made

Everything is generated by code in this folder. There's no video editor and no stock footage.

- **Animation:** every frame is drawn with the Canvas 2D API ([`@napi-rs/canvas`](https://github.com/Brooooooklyn/canvas), Skia), as a pure function of time, and piped to `ffmpeg` (H.264, 1920×1080, 30 fps).
  - `src/lib/anatomy.mjs`: the head cutaway. The tongue and soft palate are blended poses, and the airflow particles speed up where the airway narrows.
  - `src/lib/face.mjs`: the front-view face.
  - `src/explainer.mjs` and `src/exercises.mjs`: the scenes.
- **Narration:** [Kokoro-82M](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) text-to-speech (Apache 2.0), voice `af_heart`.
- **Sound:** synthesised in `src/audio.mjs`: an ambient pad and bell music bed that ducks under the voice, ticks and beeps for the timer, chimes, and a snore.
- **Timing:** `src/timeline.mjs` lays each scene out around the measured length of its narration, so picture and voice stay in sync. The exercise segments have fixed real-time durations.

### Rebuilding

Needs Node 18+, Python 3.10+ and ffmpeg.

```sh
cd videos
npm install

# Text to speech (one time): Kokoro model files from
# https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install kokoro-onnx soundfile
./build.sh /path/to/kokoro-v1.0.onnx /path/to/voices-v1.0.bin
```

`build.sh` runs these steps for both videos:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `node src/build.mjs <video> cues` | Lists the narration lines |
| `tts/speak.py` | Speaks them. It skips lines it has already spoken. |
| `node src/build.mjs <video> render --workers 4` | Renders the frames |
| `node src/build.mjs <video> audio` | Mixes the soundtrack and writes subtitles |
| `node src/build.mjs <video> mux` | Writes `out/<name>.mp4` |

`<video>` is `explainer` or `exercises`.

To check a frame without rendering everything:

```sh
node src/build.mjs exercises stills seg:ah-reps+3.2 --list
```

This writes a PNG at 3.2 s into the soft-palate repetitions to `build/exercises/stills/`. `--list` prints the timeline.
