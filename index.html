Before you start
  1. Plug in your charger. The screen has to stay on all night for the microphone to keep listening.
  2. Put the phone within about 1 m of your head, screen up, on the nightstand.
  3. Turn on Sleep or Do Not Disturb. A call or an alarm from another app pauses the recording.
  4. Turn the brightness all the way down. The night screen is black, so it gives off very little light.
  5. Don't press the side button. Locking the phone stops the recording.

      About

      Snore Log listens with the phone's microphone and checks every half second whether the last second sounded like snoring. It uses YAMNet, a sound recognition model from Google that runs on the phone. No audio or data leaves your phone.

      Loudness is shown in decibels above your room's background noise, not as an absolute sound level. Phones aren't calibrated sound meters.

      Snore Log is not a medical device. If you or a partner notice pauses in your breathing, gasping or choking at night, or you are very sleepy during the day, talk to a doctor. Those can be signs of sleep apnea, which needs a proper sleep study.

      Snore detection: YAMNet model and MediaPipe Audio, © Google, Apache License 2.0.
